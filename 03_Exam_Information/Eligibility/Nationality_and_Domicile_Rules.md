# Nationality, Citizenship, and Domicile Framework

> **Rules governing eligibility based on citizenship, regional domicile, local language mandates, and interstate reservation portability.**

## 1. Central Government & All India Services (AIS)
Under Article 309 of the Constitution of India and DoPT guidelines, candidates must be:
1. A citizen of India, OR
2. A subject of Nepal or Bhutan, OR
3. A Tibetan refugee who came over to India before January 1, 1962, with the intention of permanently settling in India, OR
4. A person of Indian origin who migrated from Pakistan, Myanmar, Sri Lanka, East African countries (Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Zambia, Malawi, Zaire, Ethiopia) or Vietnam with the intention of permanently settling.
*Note: For the Indian Administrative Service (IAS), Indian Foreign Service (IFS), and Indian Police Service (IPS), candidates must be Indian Citizens exclusively.*

## 2. State Public Service Commissions & Domicile
- **Tamil Nadu (TNPSC):** Non-domiciles may apply for open category (GT - General Turn) vacancies. However, all community reservations (BC, BCM, MBC/DC, SC, ST) are strictly restricted to candidates native to Tamil Nadu holding valid community certificates issued by competent revenue authorities in Tamil Nadu.
- **Mandatory Tamil Language Qualification:** Candidates must pass the mandatory Tamil Eligibility Test (SSLC standard, 40% qualifying marks) regardless of background or mother tongue. Candidates who did not study Tamil in school must pass the Second Class Language Test conducted by TNPSC within two years of appointment.
