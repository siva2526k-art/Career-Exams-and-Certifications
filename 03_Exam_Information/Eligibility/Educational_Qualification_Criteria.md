# Educational Qualification Criteria & Degree Equivalence

> **Authoritative guide on recognized university degrees, distance education validity, professional qualifications (CA/ICWA/CS), and final-year eligibility rules.**

## 1. Statutory Recognition of Degrees
All degrees must be recognized by statutory regulatory authorities:
- **University Grants Commission (UGC):** Mandatory recognition under Section 2(f) and 12(B) of the UGC Act, 1956.
- **All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE):** Mandatory approval for engineering, technical, and management diplomas/degrees.
- **Association of Indian Universities (AIU):** Required for determining equivalence of foreign university degrees and international qualifications.
- **Distance Education Bureau (DEB - UGC):** Degrees obtained through Open and Distance Learning (ODL) or Online mode are valid for government employment provided the institution held DEB approval during the candidate's year of admission.

## 2. Professional Qualifications Equivalent to Degree
- **Section A & B of AMIE:** Institution of Engineers (India) — recognized as equivalent to B.E./B.Tech for enrollments completed up to May 31, 2013 (subject to Supreme Court rulings and AICTE notifications).
- **Chartered Accountant (CA) / Company Secretary (CS) / Cost and Management Accountant (CMA):** Officially recognized by UGC as equivalent to a **Postgraduate Degree** for the purpose of appearing in the UGC-NET and pursuing Ph.D. admissions.

## 3. Final-Year Appearing Candidates Policy
- **UPSC CSE & Civil Services:** Candidates appearing in their final year degree examination are eligible to apply for the Preliminary Examination. However, documentary proof of passing the degree must be submitted along with the Detailed Application Form (DAF-I) before appearing in the Main Examination.
- **GATE:** Candidates currently studying in the 3rd or higher year of any undergraduate degree program, or who have already completed any government-approved degree in Engineering / Technology / Science / Commerce / Arts, are fully eligible.
- **SSC & Banking:** Candidates must possess the qualifying degree certificate or provisional certificate with marks sheet issued on or before the crucial closing date of the application form.
