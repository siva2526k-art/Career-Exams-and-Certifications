# Entry-Level Cybersecurity: CompTIA Security+ vs. CEH vs. OSCP

> **Evaluating market prestige, hands-on technical validity, cost-benefit ratio, and hiring manager perception across foundational security credentials.**

## 1. The Cybersecurity Certification Landscape
The cybersecurity certification domain suffers from significant variance between theoretical "multiple-choice" certifications and practical, performance-based certifications.

## 2. Comparison Matrix

| Parameter | CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701) | Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH v13) | OffSec Certified Professional (OSCP) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Focus** | Defensive, Governance, Core Fundamentals | Offensive concepts, tools, theory | Pure practical penetration testing |
| **Format** | 90 Questions (MCQ + PBQs), 90 Mins | 125 MCQs, 4 Hours | **24-Hour Hands-On Live Lab Exam** |
| **Cost** | **$404 USD** | **$1,199 – $1,899 USD** | **$1,699 USD (includes course + 90-day lab)** |
| **Prerequisites** | None formal (Network+ recommended) | 2 yrs experience or mandatory training | Linux CLI, scripting, networking |
| **Industry Standing** | Baseline mandatory requirement (DoD 8570) | Widely recognized in HR filters, mixed tech reputation | **Gold standard for offensive security hiring** |
| **Hands-On Reality** | Theoretical with simulated PBQs | Theoretical knowledge of hacking tools | 100% practical exploitation and reporting |
| **Expiry** | 3 Years (50 CEUs) | 3 Years (120 ECE credits) | **Lifetime (Never expires)** |

## 3. Strategic Verdict
1. **Start with CompTIA Security+:** Provides the essential foundation in cryptography, risk management, and network architecture required for any junior analyst or government role.
2. **Skip or Defer CEH:** Unless specifically mandated by an employer or government contract, CEH carries an exorbitant price tag with minimal hands-on verification compared to alternatives.
3. **Target OSCP for Offensive Roles:** If aiming for penetration testing, red teaming, or vulnerability assessment, OSCP is the definitive credential that proves authentic technical competence to engineering leads.
