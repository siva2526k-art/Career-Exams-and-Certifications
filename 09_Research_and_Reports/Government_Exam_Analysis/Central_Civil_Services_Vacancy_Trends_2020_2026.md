- **2020:** 92.51 Marks
- **2021:** 87.54 Marks
- **2022:** 88.22 Marks
- **2023:** 75.41 Marks *(Historic low due to increased CSAT analytical difficulty)*
- **2024:** 87.00 – 91.00 Marks (Normalized baseline)
- **2025–2026:** Projected between **85.00 to 92.00 Marks**.

## 3. Key Analytical Insights
1. **The 0.1% Funnel:** Out of over 1.2 million applicants, only ~0.08% achieve final selection in the prestigious All India Services (IAS, IPS, IFS).
2. **CSAT as the Gatekeeper:** The qualifying CSAT Paper-II has emerged as a primary filter, eliminating thousands of candidates despite high GS Paper-I scores.
3. **Vacancy Stability:** Following the post-COVID expansion, annual vacancies have stabilized at ~1,000–1,100 posts annually across 24 central services.
