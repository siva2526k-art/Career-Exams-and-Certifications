# SEBI Officer Grade A (Assistant Manager) – Information Technology Stream (SEBI Grade A IT)

## 1. Overview
- **Full Examination Name:** Securities and Exchange Board of India Recruitment of Officer Grade A (Assistant Manager) – Information Technology Stream
- **Short Name / Acronym:** SEBI Grade A IT
- **Examination Category:** Banking & Regulatory Bodies (Regulatory_Body)
- **Conducting Organization:** Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) (Referenced in `07_Database/organizations.json` as `ORG_SEBI`)
- **Official Website:** [https://www.sebi.gov.in](https://www.sebi.gov.in)
- **Examination Purpose:** Direct recruitment of high-caliber Computer Science and Information Technology engineers to architect, monitor, secure, and regulate technological platforms powering the Indian securities market (BSE, NSE, depositories, mutual funds, algorithmic high-frequency trading systems, and cloud infrastructure).

---

## 2. Eligibility
- **Educational Qualification:**
  - Bachelor’s Degree in Engineering (Electrical / Electronics / Electronics and Communication / Information Technology / Computer Science) from a recognized university, OR
  - Master’s in Computer Applications (MCA) from a recognized university, OR
  - Bachelor’s Degree in any discipline with a Post Graduate qualification (minimum 2 years duration) in Computers / Information Technology from a recognized university / institute.
- **Eligible Degrees / Streams:** B.E. / B.Tech (Computer Science, Information Technology, Electronics & Communication, Electrical Engineering), MCA, M.Sc. (Computer Science / IT).
- **Minimum Marks / Percentage:** Recognized degree certificate required; no restrictive minimum aggregate percentage (e.g. 60% requirement is waived, but candidate must possess genuine degree certification).
- **Age Limit:** Maximum **30 years** as of the crucial cutoff date specified in the official notification (candidate must not have been born prior to the date defined in the circular).
- **Age Relaxation:**
  - **OBC (Non-Creamy Layer):** 3 Years (Up to 33 years)
  - **SC / ST:** 5 Years (Up to 35 years)
  - **PwBD (Persons with Benchmark Disabilities):** 10 Years for General/EWS, 13 Years for OBC, 15 Years for SC/ST
  - **Ex-Servicemen:** As per statutory Government of India regulations.
- **Nationality Requirements:** Citizen of India.
- **Final-Year Student Eligibility:** **No.** Candidates must possess the qualifying degree certificate or provisional certificate with passing proof on or before the crucial closing date of online application.
- **Other Conditions / Physical Standards:** Standard medical fitness certificate issued by an authorized medical officer prior to final appointment.

## 3. Examination Pattern
The examination follows a highly competitive, 3-phase selection methodology:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Phase I: Online Screening Examination (Objective CBT)                  │
│ ├─ Paper 1: General Awareness, English, Quant, Reasoning (100 Marks)   │
│ └─ Paper 2: Specialized Computer Science / IT Subject (100 Marks)      │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Phase II: Online Merit Examination                                     │
│ ├─ Paper 1: English Descriptive Test (Drafting Skills - 100 Marks)     │
│ └─ Paper 2: Advanced IT Stream (Specialized Technical CBT - 100 Marks) │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Phase III: Interview (15% Final Weightage)                             │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Stage 1: Phase I On-Line Screening Examination
- **Mode:** Computer-Based Test (CBT).
- **Papers:**
  - **Paper 1 (Common to all streams):** Multiple-choice questions on General Awareness (including financial sector awareness), English Language, Quantitative Aptitude, and Test of Reasoning.
    - *Marks:* 100 Marks | *Duration:* 60 Minutes | *Cut-off:* Minimum 30% sectional cutoff.
  - **Paper 2 (Information Technology Stream):** Multiple-choice questions on specialized Computer Science / IT topics.
    - *Marks:* 100 Marks | *Duration:* 40 Minutes | *Cut-off:* Minimum 40% sectional cutoff.
- **Aggregate Qualifying Cutoff:** Minimum **40% aggregate** in Phase I to qualify for Phase II. Phase I marks are purely screening in nature and are **not** counted in the final merit rank.
- **Negative Marking:** 1/4th (0.25) mark deducted for each incorrect response.

### Stage 2: Phase II On-Line Merit Examination
Candidates who clear Phase I are called for Phase II (approximately 1:10 ratio of vacancies):
- **Paper 1 (General):** English (Descriptive Test) evaluating drafting skills, précis writing, essay writing, and reading comprehension.
  - *Marks:* 100 Marks | *Duration:* 60 Minutes | *Weightage:* 1/3rd (33.33%) | *Cut-off:* Minimum 30%.
- **Paper 2 (Information Technology Stream):** Advanced technical assessment (specialized technical testing covering algorithms, systems architecture, network security, database management, and hands-on coding / technical scenarios).
  - *Marks:* 100 Marks | *Duration:* 90–180 Minutes (depending on notification lab specifications) | *Weightage:* 2/3rd (66.67%) | *Cut-off:* Minimum 40%.
- **Aggregate Qualifying Threshold:** Minimum **50% aggregate** in Phase II to be eligible for shortlisting for Interview.

### Stage 3: Phase III Interview
- Shortlisted candidates are called in approximately 1:3 ratio based on aggregate Phase II marks.
- Candidates may opt for interview in Hindi or English.
- **Final Merit Computation Formula:**
  $$\text{Final Score} = (0.85 \times \text{Phase II Score}) + (0.15 \times \text{Interview Score})$$

## 4. Syllabus (Information Technology Stream)

### Phase I — Paper 2 (Specialized IT Syllabus)
1. **Algorithms & Data Structures:** Arrays, Stacks, Queues, Linked Lists, Trees (Binary Search Trees, AVL Trees, Heaps), Graphs (BFS, DFS, Minimum Spanning Trees, Dijkstra's algorithm), Sorting and Searching algorithms, Asymptotic time and space complexity ($O, \Omega, \Theta$).
2. **Database Management Systems (DBMS):** Relational algebra, SQL queries (DDL, DML, DCL), Joins, Subqueries, ER-modeling, Normalization (1NF, 2NF, 3NF, BCNF), Transaction management, ACID properties, Concurrency control, Deadlocks, Indexing, NoSQL concepts.
3. **Operating Systems:** Process and Thread management, CPU scheduling algorithms, Synchronization, Semaphores, Deadlocks (prevention, detection, avoidance), Memory management (Paging, Segmentation, Virtual Memory, Page replacement algorithms), Linux file systems and basic shell commands.
4. **Computer Networks:** OSI and TCP/IP protocol architectures, IP addressing (IPv4, IPv6, Subnetting, CIDR), Routing protocols (OSPF, BGP), Transport Layer (TCP 3-way handshake, Flow control, Congestion control, UDP), Application Layer protocols (HTTP, HTTPS, DNS, DHCP, SSH).
5. **Information & Cyber Security:** Cryptography fundamentals (Symmetric vs. Asymmetric encryption, RSA, AES, SHA hashing, Digital Signatures, PKI), Common vulnerabilities (SQL injection, XSS, CSRF, MITM, Ransomware), Firewalls, Intrusion Detection/Prevention Systems (IDS/IPS), Zero-trust architecture.
6. **Software Engineering:** SDLC models (Agile, Scrum, Waterfall), Software testing methodologies (Black-box, White-box, Unit testing, Integration testing), Object-Oriented Analysis and Design (UML diagrams, Design patterns).

### Phase II — Paper 2 (Advanced Technical & System Architecture)
- **Object-Oriented Programming & Coding:** In-depth programming concepts in Java, C++, or Python. Memory management, exception handling, multithreading, collections framework, lambda expressions, and streams.
- **Web Technologies & API Design:** RESTful API architecture, JSON/XML parsing, microservices architectures, web security tokens (JWT, OAuth 2.0).
- **Cloud Computing & Modern Infrastructure:** Virtualization, Containerization (Docker), Container orchestration (Kubernetes basics), Cloud models (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS), High availability and Disaster Recovery (DR) design.
- **Data Analytics & FinTech Architecture:** Basics of financial transaction pipelines, real-time streaming architectures (Apache Kafka, message queues), audit logs, and data integrity verification.

  - Left thumb impression (20–50 KB).
  - Hand-written declaration as per prescribed text in the notification.
- **Application Portal:** [SEBI Careers Portal](https://www.sebi.gov.in/department/human-resources-department-37/career.html)
- **Important Dates / Annual Cycle:** Notification typically released in Q1/Q2; Phase I held 4-6 weeks after notification; Phase II scheduled within 4-5 weeks of Phase I results.

## 6. Selection Process & Evaluation Rules
1. **Phase I Screening:** Strict sectional and aggregate cut-off qualification (30% Paper 1, 40% Paper 2, 40% Aggregate).
2. **Phase II Merit Shortlisting:** Candidates scoring $\ge 50\%$ aggregate are ranked based on Phase II marks (1/3rd English + 2/3rd IT Paper 2). Top candidates are invited for the Phase III Interview at ~3x vacancies.
3. **Phase III Interview & Document Verification:** Rigorous verification of original degrees, category certificates, identity proofs, and an intensive technical interview conducted by a board consisting of SEBI senior executive directors, IIT professors, and industry technology experts.
4. **Final Merit List:** Prepared by combining Phase II (85% weightage) and Interview (15% weightage).

## 7. Career Opportunities & Compensation
- **Designation:** Assistant Manager (Grade 'A') – Information Technology Stream.
- **Reporting Unit / Department:** Information Technology Department (ITD), Cyber Security & Resilience Department, Market Surveillance Cell, or Market Infrastructure Institutions Inspection Cell.
- **Key Responsibilities:**
  - Supervise and audit algorithmic trading engines, high-frequency trading (HFT) infrastructure, and exchange matching systems.
  - Formulate and enforce cybersecurity circulars and resilience guidelines for stock exchanges, clearing corporations, and depositories.
  - Implement and manage SEBI's internal enterprise cloud architecture, secure messaging protocols, and data lakes.
  - Conduct forensic technology inspections during insider trading, front-running, and market manipulation investigations.
- **Salary and Pay Scale:**
  - **Pay Scale:** Rs. 55,200 - 2,850(9) - 80,850 - EB - 2,850(2) - 86,550 - 3,300(4) - 99,750 (16 years).
  - **Monthly Gross Emoluments:** Approximately **Rs. 1,55,000/- per month** without official accommodation (or ~Rs. 1,16,000/- with residential accommodation in Mumbai).
  - **Total Cost to Company (CTC):** Approximately **Rs. 32.0 to 34.0 Lakhs per annum** including benefits, leased accommodation facility, National Pension System (NPS), Grade Allowance, Special Allowance, Family Allowance, Local Allowance, medical benefits, book grants, and reimbursement of official conveyance.
- **Career Progression Ladder:**
  - Assistant Manager (Grade A)
  - Manager (Grade B) — *Typically after 3 to 5 years*
  - Assistant General Manager (Grade C)
  - Deputy General Manager (Grade D)
  - General Manager (Grade E)
  - Chief General Manager (Grade F)
  - Executive Director (ED)
  - Whole-Time Member (WTM) / Chairman (Appointed by Government of India)
- **Posting Location:** Primarily **SEBI Head Office in Mumbai** (Bandra Kurla Complex - BKC), with limited rotational postings to regional offices in New Delhi, Kolkata, Chennai, and Ahmedabad.

## 8. Preparation Strategy & Recommended Resources
- **Algorithms & Core CS:**
  - *Data Structures and Algorithms:* Solve LeetCode / GeeksforGeeks medium-level problems on Trees, Graphs, Sorting, Dynamic Programming.
  - *Core Text:* Introduction to Algorithms by Cormen, Leiserson, Rivest, Stein (CLRS).
- **Database & SQL:**
  - *Database System Concepts* by Silberschatz, Korth, Sudarshan.
  - Practical mastery of complex SQL queries (nested subqueries, window functions, joins, indexing).
- **Computer Networks & Security:**
  - *Computer Networking: A Top-Down Approach* by Kurose and Ross.
  - *Cryptography and Network Security* by William Stallings.
- **Descriptive English (Paper 1):**
  - Daily editorial analysis in *The Hindu* or *The Indian Express*.
  - Weekly timed typing practice of précis writing (summarizing 400-word financial essays into 130 words) and formal letters/essays.
- **Mock Tests:** Full-length timed mock tests simulating the exact 40-minute Phase I Paper 2 and 60-minute descriptive English paper.

## 9. Historical Information & Trends
- **Evolution of IT Stream:** In recent years, SEBI drastically expanded the IT Cadre recruitment due to exponential growth in automated financial markets, cloud migrations, and heightened cybersecurity threats against financial market infrastructures.
- **Phase II Restructuring:** Modern cycles introduced hands-on coding and rigorous technical application testing to filter for candidates with authentic systems implementation capabilities rather than rote theoretical knowledge.
- **Cutoff Trends:**
  - Phase I: Strict statutory qualifying cutoff (40% aggregate) ensures that any candidate reaching the threshold qualifies.
  - Phase II: Cut-offs for shortlisting to interview typically hover around **60% to 68% aggregate** depending on the technical depth of Paper 2.
