# Non-Technical Popular Categories (Graduate & Undergraduate) (RRB NTPC)

## 1. Overview
- **Full Examination Name:** Non-Technical Popular Categories (Graduate & Undergraduate)
- **Short Name / Acronym:** RRB NTPC
- **Examination Category:** Railway (RRB)
- **Conducting Organization:** RRB (Referenced in `07_Database/organizations.json`)
- **Official Website:** [https://indianrailways.gov.in](https://indianrailways.gov.in)
- **Examination Purpose:** Direct competitive recruitment and entrance screening for premier careers and academic opportunities across India and internationally.

## 2. Eligibility
- **Educational Qualification:** Graduation or equivalent in relevant discipline. Recognised degree from an institution incorporated by an Act of the Central or State Legislature.
- **Eligible Degrees / Streams:** 12th pass, Undergraduate
- **Minimum Marks / Percentage:** General/OBC candidates generally require 50-60% aggregate depending on cadre rules; relaxed or pass class for SC/ST/PwBD in standard recruitment rules.
- **Age Limit:** 18 to 33 years (as of the crucial eligibility cutoff date prescribed in the annual notification).
- **Age Relaxation:**
  - **OBC (Non-Creamy Layer):** 3 Years
  - **SC / ST:** 5 Years
  - **PwBD:** 10 Years (Cumulative with category relaxation where permitted)
  - **Ex-Servicemen:** As per Central/State government statutory guidelines.
- **Nationality Requirements:** Citizen of India, subject of Nepal/Bhutan, or Tibetan refugee who came over to India before January 1, 1962, with intention of permanently settling.
- **Final-Year Student Eligibility:** No, candidates must possess the requisite degree certificate/mark sheet on or before the crucial closing date of application.
- **Other Conditions / Physical Standards:** Medical fitness according to benchmark service regulations (Shape-1 for uniformed cadres; standard medical examination for civil/administrative positions).

## 3. Examination Pattern
- **Examination Mode:** Hybrid / Computer-Based Test (CBT) or OMR Pen-and-Paper depending on stage.
- **Duration:** 2 to 3 hours per paper session.
- **Total Marks:** Varies stage-wise (typically 200–500 marks in preliminary/screening and 1000–1750 marks in descriptive mains).
- **Number of Questions:** 100 to 200 objective questions in screening phases; conventional problem-solving in mains.
- **Question Types:** Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ) with negative marking; conventional descriptive subjective essays in advanced stages.
- **Negative Marking:** Standard penalty of 1/3rd (0.33) mark or 0.25 mark deducted for each incorrect response in objective screening tests.
- **Sections / Parts:**
  - General Studies / General Awareness
  - Quantitative Aptitude & Numerical Ability
  - Reasoning & Analytical Ability
  - English Language Comprehension / Regional Language
  - Core Domain / Technical Subject Paper (where applicable)
- **Stage-wise Pattern:**
  - **Stage 1:** 1st Stage CBT (Screening)
  - **Stage 2:** 2nd Stage CBT
  - **Stage 3:** CBAT / Typing Skill Test
  - **Stage 4:** Document Verification & Medical

## 4. Syllabus
- **Complete Syllabus:**
  - **Module 1 (General Awareness & Current Affairs):** National & international events, Indian polity, constitution, economic developments, geography, history, and environmental ecology.
  - **Module 2 (Reasoning & Analytical Ability):** Syllogisms, coding-decoding, blood relations, puzzles, critical reasoning, data sufficiency.
  - **Module 3 (Quantitative Aptitude):** Number systems, algebra, arithmetic (percentages, ratio & proportion, time & work), geometry, mensuration, data interpretation.
  - **Module 4 (English & Comprehension):** Vocabulary, grammar error-spotting, reading comprehension, précis writing, sentence rearrangement.
  - **Module 5 (Core Domain / Specialized Subject):** Detailed canonical syllabus prescribed in the official notification brochure.
- **Important Subjects / High-Weightage Areas:** Core conceptual foundations, current socio-economic governance trends, and high-yield analytical problem solving.
- **Official Syllabus Document:** Available via the official notification annexure at [https://indianrailways.gov.in](https://indianrailways.gov.in).

## 5. Application
- **Application Procedure:** Entirely online via official recruitment portal. Requires One-Time Registration (OTR), entering demographic details, academic credentials, and exam center preferences.
- **Application Fee:** Rs. 100 to Rs. 1000 for General/OBC/EWS candidates; full fee exemption for Female, SC, ST, and PwBD candidates in standard government examinations.
- **Required Documents:** Scanned recent passport-sized photograph (white background), signature, photo identity proof (Aadhaar / Voter ID / Passport), degree mark sheets, and community certificates.
- **Application Portal:** [https://www.rrbapply.gov.in](https://www.rrbapply.gov.in)
- **Important Dates / Annual Cycle:** Notification released annually as part of the Annual / Periodic examination calendar.

## 6. Selection Process
- **Written Examination:** Comprehensive screening through prelims followed by rigorous mains evaluation.
- **Interview / Personality Test:** Assessment of mental alertness, critical powers of assimilation, clear and logical exposition, and intellectual integrity.
- **Physical Examination / PET / PST:** Applicable for uniformed, defence, and field-enforcement cadres (height, chest measurements, endurance run).
- **Skill Test / Typing Test:** Mandatory typing speed (30–35 wpm) or computer proficiency test where prescribed.
- **Document Verification:** In-person scrutiny of original matriculation, graduation, and reservation certificates.
- **Medical Examination:** Comprehensive medical board fitness checkup prior to appointment.
- **Final Merit List:** Prepared based strictly on aggregate marks secured in merit stages (Mains + Interview), subject to qualifying preliminary cutoffs.

## 7. Career Opportunities
- **Available Positions / Cadres:** Group A / Group B Gazetted & Non-Gazetted appointments, Officer cadres, Junior Engineer, Scientist positions.
- **Job Roles & Responsibilities:** Policy execution, public administration, technical engineering design, financial analysis, internal security management, and research development.
- **Departments / Ministries:** Government ministries, public sector enterprises, defence establishments, national laboratories.
- **Salary and Pay Scale:** 7th CPC Pay Matrix Level 7 to Level 10 (Basic Rs. 44,900 to Rs. 56,100 + DA, HRA, Transport Allowance, CGHS; initial gross monthly earnings ranging from Rs. 70,000 to Rs. 1,15,000+).
- **Career Progression / Promotion Ladder:** Structured time-bound and merit promotions leading to Joint Secretary, Chief Engineer, General Manager, or Director-level apex appointments.
- **Higher Education Opportunities:** Sponsored postgraduate degrees (M.Tech, MBA, Ph.D.), international fellowship deputations, and study leave entitlements.

## 9. Historical Information
- **Previous Examination Cycles:** Consistently conducted across regular annual cycles with high national applicant participation.
- **Previous Cutoffs:** Historical cut-offs range between 50% to 65% aggregate for preliminary qualification, fluctuating with vacancy numbers and paper difficulty.
- **Historical Vacancies:** Detailed statistical breakdown maintained in `09_Research_and_Reports/Government_Exam_Analysis/`.
- **Changes in Examination Patterns:** Transition from conventional pen-and-paper to normalized multi-shift Computer-Based Tests (CBT), introduction of negative marking, and modernized digital OTR verification.
- **Changes in Eligibility:** Implementation of EWS (Economically Weaker Sections) 10% reservation, expanded PwBD horizontal reservation categories, and revised upper age caps.
