# 01. Competitive Examinations Repository

This directory houses complete, standardized, 11-section dossiers for competitive examinations across India and international academic entryways.

## Directory Structure:
- **`Government_Exams/`**:
  - `UPSC/`: Civil Services, Engineering Services, CDS, NDA.
  - `SSC/`: CGL, CHSL, JE, MTS, CPO.
  - `Banking/`: IBPS PO/Clerk/SO, SBI PO/Clerk, RBI Grade B/Assistant, NABARD Grade A.
  - `Railway/`: RRB NTPC, JE, ALP, Group D.
  - `Defence/`: AFCAT, INET, Agniveer Schemes.
  - `State_Government/Tamil_Nadu/`: TNPSC Group I, Group II/IIA, Group IV, Combined Technical Services.
  - `Police/`: TNUSRB Sub-Inspector, TNUSRB Police Constable.
  - `Teaching/`: TN TRB Assistant Professor, UGC NET.
- **`Technical_Exams/`**:
  - `GATE/`: Computer Science & IT, Data Science & AI, Electronics & Communication.
  - `ISRO/`: Scientist/Engineer 'SC' recruitment.
  - `DRDO/`: RAC Scientist 'B' & CEPTAM STA-B.
  - `BARC/`: OCES/DGFS Scientific Officer.
  - `NIC/`: Scientist 'B' & Technical Officer.
  - `PSU_Recruitment/`: Maharatna & Navratna PSU recruitment through GATE.
- **`International_Exams/`**:
  - `GRE/`: Graduate Record Examinations General Test.
  - `GMAT/`: Graduate Management Admission Test Focus Edition.
  - `Other_Exams/`: TOEFL iBT & IELTS Academic.
