# Master Bibliography: Standard Reference Books for Competitive Exams

> **Curated list of authoritative reference textbooks for Computer Science, General Studies, Quantitative Aptitude, and Engineering.**

## 1. Computer Science & Information Technology (GATE / ISRO / DRDO)
- **Data Structures & Algorithms:**
  - *Introduction to Algorithms* by Thomas H. Cormen, Charles E. Leiserson, Ronald L. Rivest, Clifford Stein (CLRS).
  - *Data Structures and Algorithm Analysis in C* by Mark Allen Weiss.
- **Operating Systems:**
  - *Operating System Concepts* by Abraham Silberschatz, Peter B. Galvin, Greg Gagne ("Dinosaur Book").
  - *Modern Operating Systems* by Andrew S. Tanenbaum.
- **Computer Organization & Architecture:**
  - *Computer Organization and Embedded Systems* by Carl Hamacher, Zvonko Vranesic, Safwat Zaky.
  - *Computer System Architecture* by M. Morris Mano.
- **Theory of Computation:**
  - *Introduction to Automata Theory, Languages, and Computation* by John E. Hopcroft, Rajeev Motwani, Jeffrey D. Ullman.
- **Databases:**
  - *Database System Concepts* by Abraham Silberschatz, Henry F. Korth, S. Sudarshan.
- **Computer Networks:**
  - *Computer Networking: A Top-Down Approach* by James F. Kurose, Keith W. Ross.
  - *Computer Networks* by Andrew S. Tanenbaum.

## 2. General Studies & Civil Services (UPSC / State PSC)
- **Indian Polity & Constitution:** *Indian Polity* by M. Laxmikanth.
- **Modern Indian History:** *A Brief History of Modern India* by Rajiv Ahir (Spectrum).
- **Indian Economy:** *Indian Economy* by Ramesh Singh / Nitin Singhania.
- **Geography:** *Certificate Physical and Human Geography* by Goh Cheng Leong; NCERT Class 11-12 Geography.
- **Environment & Ecology:** *Environment* by Shankar IAS Academy.
- **Ethics, Integrity & Aptitude (GS IV):** *Lexicon for Ethics, Integrity & Aptitude* by Chronicle.

## 3. Quantitative Aptitude & Analytical Reasoning (Banking / SSC / RRB)
- **Quantitative Aptitude:** *Quantitative Aptitude for Competitive Examinations* by Dr. R.S. Aggarwal.
- **Advanced Maths (SSC CGL):** *Play with Advanced Maths* by Abhinay Sharma / Rakesh Yadav.
- **Verbal & Non-Verbal Reasoning:** *A Modern Approach to Verbal & Non-Verbal Reasoning* by Dr. R.S. Aggarwal.
- **Puzzles & High-Level Reasoning (Banking):** *Magical Book on Quicker Maths* by M. Tyra; *Puzzle Mania* by Oliveboard.
