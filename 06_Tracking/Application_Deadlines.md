# Urgent Application Deadlines Dashboard

> **Actionable priority dashboard highlighting closing dates for online registration, fee submission, and document correction windows.**

## 🔴 High Urgency (Closing Within 15 Days)
* *Check official recruitment portals weekly for active gazette notices.*
* **TNPSC Portal:** Ensure One-Time Registration (OTR) is renewed and Aadhaar linking is authenticated.
* **SSC Portal:** Download the modern MySSC app for real-time live selfie capture and biometric validation.

## 🟡 Medium Urgency (Closing Within 30 Days)
* **National Testing Agency (NTA):** Verify category certificate validity (OBC-NCL and EWS certificates must be issued on or after April 1 of the ongoing financial year).
* **UPSC OTR:** Updates to demographic profiles in OTR must be completed before the application form submission window closes.

## 🟢 Regular Annual Cadence
- **January – March:** UPSC CSE, TNPSC Annual Planner, SSC GD Constable.
- **April – June:** SSC CHSL, UPSC CDS II, AFCAT II, State Engineering Services.
- **July – September:** SSC CGL, IBPS PO/Clerk, GATE Application Window Opens (GOAPS).
- **October – December:** SBI PO, RBI Grade B, Defence Rallies, UGC NET Winter Cycle.
