# 06. Active Operational Tracking Dashboards

Dynamic dashboards tracking exam cycles, deadlines, and recertification windows:
- **`Upcoming_Exams.md`**: Live timeline of active and upcoming recruitment cycles.
- **`Application_Deadlines.md`**: Actionable urgency tracker (15-day and 30-day deadlines).
- **`Annual_Exam_Calendar.md`**: Master recurring calendar from January to December.
- **`Certification_Expiry.md`**: Continuing education (CPE/CEU) and recertification requirements.
- **`Notification_Tracker.md`**: Gazette releases and statutory circular tracking.
