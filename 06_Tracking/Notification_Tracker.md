# Official Notification Tracker & Gazette Archive

> **Continuously indexed log of primary employment notifications, gazette extraordinary issues, and recruitment rule amendments.**

### Standard Verification Hierarchy:
1. **The Gazette of India (Extraordinary / Weekly):** Primary statutory legal authority published by Department of Publication, Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (`egazette.gov.in`).
2. **Tamil Nadu Government Gazette:** Published by the Directorate of Stationery and Printing, Chennai (`stationeryprinting.tn.gov.in`).
3. **Official Commission Portals:** `.gov.in` and `.nic.in` domains directly belonging to conducting bodies.
4. **Employment News / Rozgar Samachar:** Weekly official government recruitment journal.

### Recent Regulatory Updates Tracked:
- **UPSC:** Mandatory facial recognition and AI-enabled attendance verification implemented across all civil services and defence exam centers.
- **SSC:** Transition to the new unified `ssc.gov.in` digital platform with live-photograph capture replacing static scanned photo uploads.
- **GATE:** Permanent addition of the **Data Science & Artificial Intelligence (DA)** test paper, allowing candidates to appear in two test papers simultaneously.
- **TNPSC:** Single-window annual recruitment calendar framework with strict adherence to the Tamil Eligibility Test (qualifying 40% threshold).
