# 04. Career Pathways & Compensation Frameworks

Systematic mapping of educational degrees and test qualifications to real-world employment:
- **`Career_Eligibility_Matrix.md`**: Master cross-mapping from 10th pass to working professionals.
- **`Government_Jobs/`**: Group A/B/C classification, 7th Central Pay Commission (CPC) Pay Matrix.
- **`PSU_Jobs/`**: Industrial DA (IDA) scales (E-2 to E-9), Maharatna executive trainee compensation.
- **`Private_Sector/`**: Modern tech career ladders, SDE levels, cloud architecture market rates.
- **`Research_Organizations/`**: Flexible Complementing Scheme (FCS), Scientist-B to Apex Secretary ladders.
- **`International_Careers/`**: Global engineering mobility and overseas graduate research pathways.
