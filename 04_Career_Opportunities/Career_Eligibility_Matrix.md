# Career Eligibility Mapping Matrix

> **A systematic, qualification-first decision matrix connecting: Educational Qualification → Competitive Examination → Professional Certification → Industry Skills → Actual Job Opportunities.**

---

## ⚠️ Critical Rule: Qualification Eligibility vs. Recruitment Reality
* **Qualification Eligibility:** The statutory academic minimum required merely to apply for an examination (e.g., any recognized Bachelor's degree for UPSC CSE).
* **Recruitment Reality:** The actual competitive bar (e.g., multi-stage percentiles, physical fitness standards, technical interviews, category cutoffs, and background verification) required to secure an appointment.
* **Certifications:** Professional certifications (e.g., AWS, CCNA, CKA) **demonstrate practical competence** and accelerate private-sector hiring or PSU interviews; they **do not automatically guarantee appointment** in government services without clearing the statutory competitive process.
