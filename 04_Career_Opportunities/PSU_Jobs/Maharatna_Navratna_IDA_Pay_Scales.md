# Public Sector Undertakings (PSU) Industrial DA (IDA) Pay Structures

> **Analysis of Executive Trainee recruitment, IDA pay scales, Performance Related Pay (PRP), and Superannuation benefits in Maharatna and Navratna CPSEs.**

## 1. Classification of Central Public Sector Enterprises (CPSEs)
- **Maharatna:** ONGC, IOCL, NTPC, BHEL, GAIL, SAIL, CIL, POWERGRID, BPCL, HPCL, REC, PFC, OIL.
- **Navratna:** BEL, HAL, NMDC, NBCC, Engineers India Limited (EIL), NALCO, Rashtriya Ispat Nigam Limited (RINL).
- **Miniratna:** BEML, Cochin Shipyard, IRCTC, RailTel, Antrix, etc.

## 3. Total CTC Breakdown & Perks
Unlike Central Government posts governed by the 7th CPC, PSU executive compensation includes substantial corporate components:
1. **Basic Pay + Industrial DA (revised quarterly).**
2. **Cafeteria Perks (up to 35% of Basic Pay):** Medical, professional development, club membership, fitness, vehicle fuel, mobile allowance.
3. **Performance Related Pay (PRP):** Variable annual bonus ranging from 40% to 150% of Basic Pay based on individual Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) performance scores and enterprise profitability.
4. **Superannuation Package (30% of Basic + DA):** Contributory Provident Fund, Gratuity, Post-Retirement Medical Scheme (PRMS), and Pension.
*Total Annual CTC for an Executive Trainee typically ranges from **Rs. 18.0 to 24.5 Lakhs per annum**.*
