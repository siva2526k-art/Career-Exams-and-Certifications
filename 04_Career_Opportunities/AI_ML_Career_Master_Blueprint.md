## 💼 3. Specialized AI & ML Job Roles & Tech Stacks

### 1. Machine Learning Engineer (MLE)
* **What They Do:** Bridges the gap between data science prototypes and production software. Builds, trains, optimizes, and maintains production ML pipelines.
* **Core Tech Stack:** Python, PyTorch / TensorFlow, Scikit-Learn, Docker, MLflow, FastAPI, SQL, Redis.
* **Key Metrics:** Model accuracy, latency (p99 < 50ms), throughput, model drift detection.

### 2. Computer Vision Engineer
* **What They Do:** Develops visual intelligence for autonomous driving, satellite imagery, facial recognition, and industrial inspection.
* **Core Tech Stack:** OpenCV, PyTorch, YOLOv8/v10, Detectron2, Segment Anything (SAM), CUDA, TensorRT.
* **Key Metrics:** Mean Average Precision (mAP), Frames Per Second (FPS) on edge devices.

### 3. NLP & Generative AI / LLM Engineer
* **What They Do:** Builds conversational agents, enterprise RAG systems, document intelligence, and domain-adapted LLMs.
* **Core Tech Stack:** Hugging Face Transformers, LangChain, LlamaIndex, vLLM, Vector Databases (Pinecone, Weaviate, Qdrant), LoRA/QLoRA.
* **Key Metrics:** Context retrieval accuracy, BLEU/ROUGE scores, token-per-second generation rate, hallucination minimization.

### 4. MLOps Engineer (Machine Learning Operations)
* **What They Do:** Automates the lifecycle of machine learning models (continuous training, testing, deployment, and monitoring).
* **Core Tech Stack:** Kubernetes, KubeFlow, Amazon SageMaker, DVC (Data Version Control), Prometheus, Grafana, Terraform.
* **Key Metrics:** Zero-downtime canary deployments, training cluster GPU utilization efficiency.

### 5. AI Research Scientist
* **What They Do:** Invents novel neural network architectures, publishes at top conferences (NeurIPS, ICML, CVPR), and pushes algorithmic frontiers.
* **Core Tech Stack:** Deep theoretical mathematics, custom CUDA kernels, PyTorch, distributed multi-node GPU training (DeepSpeed, Megatron-LM).
* **Qualification:** Typically requires an M.Tech / M.S. or Ph.D. from premier institutes (IISc, IITs, or top global universities).

   - **Work:** Autonomous military drone swarms, target tracking vision algorithms, battlefield NLP systems.

   - **Exam:** **ISRO ICRB Centralised Recruitment Exam** (CBT + Interview).

   - **Pay:** 7th CPC Level 10 (Gross: **~₹1,15,000/month** + PRIS performance bonuses).
3. **SEBI (Securities & Exchange Board of India):**
   - **Exam:** **SEBI Grade A IT Examination** (Phase I CBT + Phase II Technical Coding/Systems + Interview).
   - **Work:** Algorithmic high-frequency trading surveillance, deep neural nets detecting market insider trading.

4. **The Gateway: GATE DA (Data Science & AI):**
   - Written test paper by IITs/IISc directly covering: Probability & Statistics, Linear Algebra, Calculus, Python DS, Machine Learning (Supervised/Unsupervised), and Artificial Intelligence (Search, Logic).

## 🏢 5. Top MNC & International Recruiters

The AI & ML private job market is tiered into 5 distinct categories of global recruiters:

### A. Foundation Model & Core AI Research Labs (Apex Tier)
* **Companies:** **OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Microsoft Research (MSR), Meta FAIR (Fundamental AI Research), Anthropic, Mistral AI**.
* **What They Look For:** Top-tier algorithmic depth, contributions to open-source model repositories (Hugging Face), publications in NeurIPS/CVPR, custom attention mechanisms, extreme GPU cluster optimization.
* **Compensation:** **$250,000 – $600,000+** (US) | **₹50 – 1.2 Crore+** (India R&D Centers).

### B. Big Tech & Global Cloud Platforms (Tier 1)
* **Companies:** **Google, Microsoft, Amazon (AWS AI), Apple, Meta, Adobe**.
* **What They Look For:** Large-scale distributed systems, enterprise recommendation engines (YouTube, Amazon Retail), voice assistants (Siri, Alexa), multimodal generative systems.
* **Compensation:** **₹35 – 75 LPA** for SDE-II / ML Engineers in India.

### C. Semiconductor & AI Compute Hardware Giants
* **Companies:** **NVIDIA, AMD, Qualcomm, Intel, Tenstorrent, Broadcom**.
* **What They Look For:** CUDA C++, low-level hardware-aware neural network acceleration, TensorRT, compiler optimization, on-device mobile AI (Snapdragon NPU).
* **Compensation:** **₹30 – 65 LPA** in India.

### D. Autonomous Driving, Robotics & Aerospace
* **Companies:** **Tesla (Autopilot), Waymo, Cruise, Boston Dynamics, Zoox, Rivian**.
* **What They Look For:** Real-time sensor fusion (LiDAR, Radar, Cameras), SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping), end-to-end vision-action neural networks.
* **Compensation:** Top-of-market equity + base salary packages.

### E. Quantitative Trading & FinTech AI (Highest Cash Compensation)
* **Companies:** **Jane Street, Citadel Securities, Jump Trading, Tower Research Capital, Two Sigma, Hudson River Trading (HRT)**.
* **What They Look For:** Mathematical olympiad caliber, high-frequency time-series predictive modeling, low-latency C++/Python execution, statistical arbitrage neural networks.
* **Compensation:** **₹1.0 – 2.5 Crore+** initial CTC for top-tier engineers.
