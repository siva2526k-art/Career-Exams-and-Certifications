## 2. In-Hand Monthly Gross Estimation Formula
$$\text{Gross Salary} = \text{Basic Pay} + \text{DA} + \text{HRA} + \text{Transport Allowance (TA)} + \text{DA on TA}$$

- **Dearness Allowance (DA):** Periodically revised biannually based on the All India Consumer Price Index (AICPIN).
- **House Rent Allowance (HRA):**
  - **Class X Cities (Metros: Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Hyderabad):** 30% of Basic Pay (or updated ceiling).
  - **Class Y Cities (State Capitals & Major Urban Centers):** 20% of Basic Pay.
  - **Class Z Cities (Rural and Small Towns):** 10% of Basic Pay.
