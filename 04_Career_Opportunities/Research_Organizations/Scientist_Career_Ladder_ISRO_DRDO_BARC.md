# Scientific Research Career Hierarchy: ISRO, DRDO, BARC & CSIR

> **The career trajectory, promotion mechanisms (Flexible Complementing Scheme), and research mandate in India's premier scientific institutions.**

## 1. Flexible Complementing Scheme (FCS)
In conventional civil administration, promotions are strictly vacancy-dependent. In premier scientific institutions (Department of Space / ISRO, DRDO, Department of Atomic Energy / BARC, and CSIR), promotions up to the level of Scientist 'H' are governed by the **Flexible Complementing Scheme (FCS)**:
- Promotions are based on **individual merit, research publications, patented innovations, and project assessments**, independent of vacancies.
- Candidates become eligible for assessment after completing prescribed residency periods (typically 3 to 5 years per grade).

## 3. Special Scientific Allowances
- **Performance Related Incentive Scheme (PRIS):** Monthly and annual incentive payments linked to organizational milestones (satellite launches, missile trials, reactor criticality).
- **Professional Update Allowance:** Annual grant of Rs. 22,500 to Rs. 45,000 for acquiring research literature, scientific memberships, and computing hardware.
